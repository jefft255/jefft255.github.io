Forest mapping project

April 17, 2019

Quick facts

  • 1.4 hectares of forest mapped with a Clearpath Husky and a Velodyne HDL-32E
  • 943 trees were manually segmented in point clouds, and their diameter and species was identified on the field.
  • Biggest dataset for 3D forest mapping and tree diameter measurements
  • We tested various diameter estimation methods
  • Part of the Automated Forestry and Logging Operations project
  • Published and presented at the 12th Conference on Field and Service Robotics and the Journal of Field Robotics
